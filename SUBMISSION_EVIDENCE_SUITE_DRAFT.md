## Summary

Evidence Integrity Suite is a catalog of ten reusable GenLayer Intelligent Contract patterns for web-grounded decisions. It extends beyond binary claim verification into corroboration quorum, contradiction analysis, citation provenance, source-independence checks, policy compliance, challengeable attestations, material-change monitoring, action routing and auditable decision receipts.

## Novelty / complexity / impact

- **Novelty:** treats evidence quality itself as on-chain decision material: independence, conflict, material drift and abstention are first-class outputs.
- **Complexity:** multiple standalone contracts use live web access, structured LLM judgments, validator re-execution and deterministic safety thresholds.
- **Impact:** reusable building blocks for marketplaces, escrows, agents, compliance workflows, monitoring and governance.
